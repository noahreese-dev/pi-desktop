# Probe: find out what the learner already knows

New ideas are understood through the ones a learner already holds, so start from what is there. Teaching too low wastes
their time; teaching too high produces memorized words without understanding. A probe maps the ground before the
first lesson, and again whenever a new part of the mission starts.

## Plan the map

From `MISSION.md`, list the 3-6 strands of knowledge the goal depends on (for a goal like "read restaurant menus in
Spanish": food vocabulary, numbers and prices, basic noun-adjective agreement, common menu phrases). For each strand,
write down for yourself what a beginner, an intermediate and a strong answer would look like.

## Ask, one question at a time

- **One question per message.** Wait for the answer before the next. Never send a questionnaire.
- **Start in the middle of a strand,** then move down if they struggle or up if they're comfortable. Two or three
  questions per strand are usually enough to find the edge.
- **Ask them to produce, not to recognize:** "how would you work this out?" or "explain it as you would to a friend"
  reveals more than a yes/no or multiple choice question.
- **Ask how sure they are** now and then. Confident and wrong is a misconception worth recording; unsure and right
  is fragile knowledge worth reinforcing.
- **Accept "I don't know"** as useful data, and say so, so they don't guess. Don't teach during the probe beyond a
  one-line correction when they ask.
- Keep it short: a probe should take a few minutes, not a session. If they say they already know a strand, ask one
  question that would confirm it, then take their word and note the claim.

## Record it

Write one learning record per probe (see LEARNING-RECORD-FORMAT.md) with, for each strand:

- **Floor:** what they reliably hold.
- **Edge:** where answers became uncertain; this is where teaching starts.
- **Misconceptions:** wrong ideas held with confidence, in their words.

Then tell the learner in two or three lines what you found and where the first lesson will start.
